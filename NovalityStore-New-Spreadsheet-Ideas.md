# New Spreadsheet Ideas for NovalityStore

**Research date:** October 6, 2026  
**Goal:** Identify spreadsheet products that are not currently visible in the NovalityStore shop and show promising Etsy demand signals.

## Important qualification

These are **opportunity ideas, not guaranteed best sellers**. Etsy search pages show demand and competition signals, not reliable sales volume. Before building all eight, validate the idea with an original sample dashboard, a clear buyer, and a small test listing.

I compared these ideas against the shop's current visible catalog, which already includes personal finance, family budget, lifestyle, wedding, travel, Etsy seller, craft-fair, catering, bakery, construction, school, church, rental property, gift tracker, Secret Santa, and general bookkeeping spreadsheets.

## Recommended publishing order

1. Pet Sitting and Dog Walking Business Manager
2. Social Media Content Calendar and Analytics Planner
3. Automated Meal Planner and Grocery List
4. Fitness and Weight Loss Tracker
5. Cleaning Business CRM and Pricing Calculator
6. Handmade Product Pricing and Profit Calculator
7. Client CRM and Lead Tracker for Freelancers
8. Home Maintenance and Appliance Warranty Tracker

The first five have the clearest Etsy search-market evidence from the research completed. The last three are promising niche extensions but should be validated with Etsy search autocomplete and competing listing review counts before production.

## Best 3 to Build First

### 1. Pet Sitting Business Manager

Best combination of specific buyer, high price, and strong Etsy search evidence.

### 2. Social Media Content Planner for Etsy Sellers

Can attract Etsy sellers and creators while fitting your existing digital-product audience.

### 3. Automated Meal Planner

Large visible demand, but competition is high. It must include automation and useful functions instead of only blank cells.

## Product Validation Before Building

For each idea:

1. Search Etsy using the exact primary phrase.
2. Record the first 20 organic listing titles and review counts.
3. Note repeated buyer benefits, not competitor wording.
4. Check whether the top listings have weak screenshots, poor instructions, or missing features.
5. Build a small working prototype with 3–5 tabs.
6. Ask 3–5 target users to test it.
7. Publish only when formulas, filters, and instructions work.

## Final Advice

Do not make all eight products at once. Build the **Pet Sitting**, **Social Media Content**, and **Meal Planner** templates first, publish them with professional screenshots and instructions, and measure views, favorites, and sales for 2–3 weeks. If one gets views but no sales, improve the thumbnail, first 160 characters of the description, price, and included-feature list before creating more versions.
