## Executive Recommendation

Do not publish random products. Build two tightly organized collections:

1. **Seasonal crochet patterns:** Christmas ornaments, quick beginner projects, and small amigurumi that can be finished quickly or sold at craft fairs.
2. **High-value spreadsheet templates:** 2027 budgeting, holiday spending, small-business bookkeeping, Etsy seller tracking, and event planning.

The shop currently has two audiences. Keep the categories clearly separated with shop sections, consistent thumbnails, and different listing images. Consider a separate spreadsheet shop in the future if the audiences continue to behave differently.

## Listing Quality Requirements

### Crochet

- Photograph the actual finished item.
- Test each pattern yourself and with at least one independent tester.
- State US or UK terminology.
- State hook size, yarn weight, yarn quantity, finished size, and skill level.
- Show a page from the actual PDF.
- State what is included and that no physical item is shipped.
- Never use an AI image as the claimed result of the pattern.

### Spreadsheets

- Show the dashboard and each major tab.
- Include both Excel and Google Sheets versions only if both are actually tested.
- Include sample data so buyers understand the product.
- Include a blank copy and a quick-start guide.
- Explain formulas, compatibility, and mobile limitations.
- State whether Microsoft 365, Excel desktop, or Google Sheets is required.
- Include a clear digital-download notice.
- Do not claim tax, legal, or accounting compliance unless qualified to do so.

## Sales and Bundle Offers

Use normal prices that communicate value. Run launch promotions for 5–7 days instead of leaving every item permanently discounted.

Recommended offers:

- Buy any 2 crochet patterns, save 15%.
- Buy any 3 crochet patterns, save 25%.
- Christmas bundle launch: 20–25% off for one week.
- Spreadsheet bundle: 20% below the combined individual price.
- Add a free one-page quick-start guide to increase perceived value.

## Risks and Important Notes

- Etsy demand changes quickly; search results are not a guarantee of sales.
- High-review competitors may outrank a new listing, so quality images and a specific niche are essential.
- Do not copy competitor products, designs, photos, or wording.
- Digital products still need accurate instructions and customer support.
- A negative or misleading listing review can damage conversion. Correct the underlying product issue before increasing advertising.
